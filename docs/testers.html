Early testers

Help us launch Hmong Date

Google Play needs a small group of real people to try Hmong Date for 14 days before it can go public. You'll get the app early and free, and your feedback shapes what we build next.

You need an Android phone and a Gmail (Google) account.

  1. Join the tester group
    Sign in with the Gmail you use on your Android phone, then tap Join group.
    Join the tester group
  2. Accept the test invite
    Open this link with the same Gmail and tap Become a tester. It can take a few minutes after joining the group to work.
    Become a tester
  3. Install the app
    Download Hmong Date from Google Play and create your profile.
    Get it on Google Play
  4. Keep it installed for 14 days
    Open it every few days, swipe, chat and tell us what you think. Please don't uninstall before the 14 days are up.

Questions or feedback?

Email support@hmongdate.com. Thank you for supporting the Hmong community!
