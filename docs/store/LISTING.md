## Full description (max 4000)
Hmong Date is the dating app made for the Hmong community. Whether you're looking for a long-term partner, a future husband or wife, or someone who understands your family and traditions, Hmong Date helps you meet Hmong singles near you and across the country.

WHY HMONG DATE
• Built for our community — clans, dialects (Hmong Daw and Hmong Leng), hometowns and traditions are part of every profile.
• Meet people who get it — family first, New Year festivals, pov pob, Hmong food and everything in between.
• Safe and respectful — photo verification, block and report tools, and a team that reviews every report.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create your profile with photos, your clan, dialect, hometown and what you're looking for.
• Discover — swipe through Hmong singles. Like someone, and if they like you back, it's a match!
• Interests — browse people by what matters to you: Hmong Tradition, Family First, Hmong Food, Hmong Sports, faith, education, state and more.
• Chat — send messages and photos, react to messages, and see when someone is typing or has read your message.
• Likes — see who liked you and like them back to match instantly.

STAY IN CONTROL
• Choose who you want to see: men, women or everyone.
• Hide your active status or read receipts anytime.
• Unmatch or block anyone, any time — and undo it from Settings.
• Delete your account and data whenever you want, right in the app.

HMONG DATE UNLIMITED (optional)
Free members get daily likes, views and rewinds. Upgrade to Unlimited for unlimited likes, see everyone who liked you, unlimited rewinds and Interests browsing, message anyone, and spot new members with the NEW badge. Subscriptions renew monthly until cancelled in Google Play.

You must be 18 or older to use Hmong Date.
