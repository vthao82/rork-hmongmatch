Account Deletion

Delete My Account

We're sorry to see you go. This page lets you permanently delete your Hmong Date account and personal data.

Fastest way: if you still have the app installed, tap Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is instant.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, age, photos, bio, preferences).
  • Your swipes, likes, and matches.
  • Your uploaded photos and verification selfie (if any).
  • Your account login credentials.
  • Your push notification token.

What we retain (and for how long)

  • Messages you already sent may remain visible to the recipient, labeled as from a "deleted user".
  • A minimal record (email hash + deletion date) is kept for up to 12 months to prevent abuse and comply with legal obligations.
  • Backups are purged on a rolling 90-day cycle.

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us at support@hmongdate.com with:

  • The email address associated with your Hmong Date account.
  • The subject line: "Delete my Hmong Date account".

We will confirm deletion within 30 days.

Or fill out this form

We'll email you a confirmation link and process the deletion once verified.

By submitting this form, an email will be sent from your device's default mail app to our support team. If you don't have a mail app configured, please write directly to support@hmongdate.com.
